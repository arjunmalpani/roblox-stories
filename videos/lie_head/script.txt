# lie_head: "In this world, every time you lie, your head gets bigger."
# Narrator: Eddie (13). Rival: Principal Carl. Best friend: Leo.
# Word count: ~370 words. Target length: ~85s @ 258 WPM.

My head was so gigantic, the fire department had to cut the classroom roof off with a chainsaw just to pull me out!
And it was still growing, with twenty students pushing on my forehead!
You see, in this world, every time you tell a lie, your head expands by ten percent.
And every time you tell the truth, it shrinks back down.
That's why nobody ever lies on their homework!
Kids walk into school confessing their darkest secrets just to fit through the front door.
My dad once lied about doing the dishes and got his head wedged in the refrigerator for two days!
My name is Eddie, and I was the most honest kid in town.
Until last Tuesday, when I accidentally broke the school's golden trophy with a stray dodgeball!
Principal Carl sprinted into the hallway!
"Who smashed the trophy?!"
Panic hit me. "A raccoon did it!" I blurted.
Poof!
My head swelled to the size of a giant pumpkin!
Carl narrowed his eyes. "A raccoon in the cafeteria?!"
"Yes! And it was wearing a tiny cape!"
Boom!
My head expanded again! Now it was the size of a golf cart!
My neck was wobbling! My balance was completely gone!
"Eddie, are you lying to me?!" shouted Carl.
"No! I love honesty! Honesty is my middle name!"
Bam!
My head exploded to the size of a minivan, wedging tight between the lockers!
Kids were screaming! Lockers were crumpling like soda cans!
Carl backed away in horror. "He's going to demolish the entire school! Call the SWAT team!"
Meanwhile, ceiling tiles were cracking! Concrete dust was crumbling down around my ears!
If I lied one more time, my head would take out the whole building!
Then I noticed Carl's shiny toupee slipping sideways in the chaos.
Truth! The rule works both ways!
I took a deep breath and screamed at the top of my lungs:
"I DON'T ACTUALLY LIKE BROCCOLI!"
Pop! My head shrunk ten percent!
"I COPIED LEO'S MATH HOMEWORK IN THIRD GRADE!"
Pop! Down to beach ball size!
"AND PRINCIPAL CARL'S HAIR IS ACTUALLY A CHEAP FAKE WIG!"
Pop! Pop! Pop!
My head zipped right back to normal size! I squeezed loose and hit the floor!
The entire cafeteria erupted into cheers!
Carl turned bright red and slapped both hands over his bald head.
I walked out of school feeling like an absolute champion.
Until my mom called my phone.
"Eddie, did you eat the last chocolate cupcake?"
I looked at the phone.
"No, Mom."
Boing!
