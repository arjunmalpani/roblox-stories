# hiccup_shrink - "In this world, every time you hiccup, you shrink"
# Formula: hook (result first) -> rule -> 3 society examples -> "But" pivot -> escalation ladder
# (tent clothes, sneaker size, chili bowl, dog tongue, flea size) -> "Then I noticed something" -> payoff -> button.
I hiccuped in class and shrank to the size of a pencil! Then my teacher dropped a textbook straight toward my head!
You see, in this world, every time you hiccup, you shrink by half your size. That's why soda is banned in thirty countries. Roller coasters have toddler-and-ant seatbelts. And my uncle hiccuped five times at his wedding, so his bride carried him in her purse.
But I have chronic allergies and I love fizzy soda. And yesterday, the school bully, Crusher Carl, dared me to chug a two-liter Mega-Fizz in front of the whole cafeteria.
I slammed the empty bottle down. Everyone cheered! Then my throat tickled.
Hic! Poof! My clothes collapsed into a giant circus tent. I was three feet tall!
Hic! Poof! Now I was the size of a sneaker!
Carl laughed and pointed. Look at the tiny bug! He slammed his giant boot down to stomp me!
Stomp! I dodged, but the shockwave threw me through the air. Splat! Right into a bowl of cafeteria chili.
Hic! Poof! Now I was two inches tall! The lunch lady picked up the ladle with me in it!
She dumped the chili onto Buster’s tray, the school guard dog. Buster sniffed. He growled. His giant pink tongue came swooping down! Whoosh!
Hic! Poof! I shrank again! Now I was the size of a flea, clinging to Buster's wet nose!
Buster took a giant breath. He was about to sneeze!
Then I noticed something. In our world, if hiccups shrink you... scaring away hiccups grows you back instantly! And Buster’s nose was covered in black pepper from the chili!
I crawled into Buster's nostril and tickled his nose hair. Ah... ah... CHOO!
The sneeze launched me like a rocket straight at Crusher Carl's face. Carl saw a flying speck heading for his eyeball and screamed in absolute terror! AAAHHH!
The scream scared my hiccups away! BOOM! I expanded in mid-air like an airbag!
I slammed into Carl, knocking him headfirst into the trash can. The whole cafeteria erupted in cheers!
Carl climbed out of the trash, covered in old noodles. He glared at me, trembling with rage. Then Carl hiccuped.
Hic! Poof! Carl shrank to the size of a teacup.
Buster the dog walked over, wagging his tail.
Sit, tiny Carl. Good boy.
